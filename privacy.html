Snapledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy describes what Snapledger does with your information. It is written to match what the app actually does today. If that changes, this page changes with it.

Who we are

Snapledger is operated by Ishaan Chauhan. You can reach us at sales.quoteback@gmail.com.

What we collect

Receipt photos. When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to read the text on it. We do not keep a copy of the photo on our server after the response is returned.

Receipt data. The merchant, date, line items and amounts read from your receipt are stored on your device only. We never receive them as data, and no amount from a receipt appears in the usage events described below. The photo itself is sent to be read, as described above, and is not kept afterwards.

Your budgets and categories. Stored on your device only. The total of the budgets you set during setup is included in a usage event, so we can tell whether people finish setting one up. Individual category budgets are not.

A random installation identifier. A random string generated on first launch, used to apply usage limits fairly and to group usage events. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number, and it changes if you reinstall the app.

Usage events. Events such as when a scan starts, whether it succeeded, which budget category a receipt was filed under, and how confident the reading was. These are tied to the random installation identifier above.

Standard app and device information, attached automatically to those usage events: app version and build, operating system and version, device model, manufacturer and type, language, timezone and screen size. This is used to diagnose problems, such as scanning failing on a particular phone. Location lookup is switched off, so we do not receive a city or country.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store your name, email address or phone number
  • We do not use Android's automatic backup, so your receipts are not copied to

Google Drive. Nothing on your phone is synced anywhere unless you export it yourself

  • We do not collect your location, and we switch off the location lookup our

analytics provider would otherwise perform from your IP address

  • We do not access your contacts, photos beyond the ones you choose to scan, or

any other app's data

  • We do not have accounts, so we hold no login credentials
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising

Who else processes your data

Anthropic processes receipt photos to extract the text. Their handling is covered by their own privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Railway hosts our server, and processes requests in transit.

PostHog receives the usage events and app information described above. It does not receive receipt photos or any amount from a receipt.

That is the complete list.

How long we keep things

Receipt photos are held only for the seconds it takes to read them, then discarded. Everything else lives on your device, under your control.

Your choices

Delete everything. Settings → Erase all data removes every receipt, photo and budget from your device immediately and permanently.

Delete one receipt. Open any receipt and choose Delete.

Take your data with you. Settings → Export receipts writes every receipt to a CSV file and hands it to whatever app you choose. Nothing is backed up anywhere - not by us and not to your Google Drive - so this export is the only copy that exists off your phone. If losing the app would matter to you, export it somewhere safe.

Delete your usage events. Settings shows an Installation ID. Email it to sales.quoteback@gmail.com and we will delete the usage events recorded against it. That identifier is the only thing those events are attached to.

Stop all collection. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device, permanently - there is no backup to restore from. Erasing all data in Settings also issues a new Installation ID, which detaches you from anything recorded before.

We hold no account for you, so there is nothing else to delete or export.

Children

Snapledger is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be surfaced in the app.
