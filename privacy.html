Snapledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy describes what Snapledger does with your information. It is written

to match what the app actually does today. If that changes, this page changes

with it.

Before monetizing: once the app offers paid plans, Google Play requires

a physical address that is displayed publicly on the store listing. Decide

what address that will be before switching on subscriptions - a home

address becomes searchable once it is there.

Who we are

Snapledger is operated by Ishaan Chauhan. You can reach us at

sales.quoteback@gmail.com.

What we collect

Receipt photos. When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent over an

encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API

to read the text on it. We do not keep a copy of the photo on our server after

the response is returned.

Receipt data. The merchant, date, line items, totals and category read from

your receipt are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded to us.

Your budgets and categories. Stored on your device only.

A random installation identifier. A random string generated on first launch,

used to apply usage limits fairly and to group usage events. It is not linked

to your name, email, phone number or device identifiers, and it changes if you

reinstall the app.

Usage events. Anonymous events such as when a scan starts, whether it

succeeded, and which screens are used. These are tied to the random

installation identifier described above.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store your name, email address or phone number
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos beyond the ones you choose to scan, or

any other app's data

  • We do not have accounts, so we hold no login credentials
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising

Who else processes your data

Anthropic processes receipt photos to extract the text. Their handling is

covered by their own privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Railway hosts our server, and processes requests in transit.

That is the complete list.

How long we keep things

Receipt photos are held only for the seconds it takes to read them, then

discarded. Everything else lives on your device, under your control.

Your choices

Delete everything. Settings → Erase all data removes every receipt, photo

and budget from your device immediately and permanently.

Delete one receipt. Open any receipt and choose Delete.

Stop all collection. Uninstalling the app removes everything, because your

data is stored on the device rather than on our servers.

Because we do not hold your data on a server and cannot identify you, we have

no account to delete on our side and nothing to export to you.

Children

Snapledger is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect

information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes

will be surfaced in the app.
